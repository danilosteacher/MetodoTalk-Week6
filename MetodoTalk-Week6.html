Lembra do que você aprendeu?
I/You (W4) → He/She (W6) — só muda o pronome e o verbo!
I/You → He/She · Adicione -S ou -ES no verbo!
I manage the team.→He manages the team.
I travel to farms.→She travels to farms.
I coordinate logistics.→He coordinates logistics.
I check the fields.→She checks the fields.
My main role is...→His/Her main role is...
Regra: Com He e She, o verbo sempre recebe -S (manages, travels) ou -ES (checks, coordinates). Com I e You, o verbo fica igual. Simples assim!

Situação: AgroBrasil precisa preparar um relatório de equipe para o board australiano. Ana e James estão numa reunião de planejamento discutindo o que cada membro da equipe FAZ na operação.

JAMES: "And who handles logistics?"

JAMES: "Does she work at the headquarters?"

JAMES: "What about the field technician?"
ANA: "Felipe. He doesn't work at the office at all — he is always in the field. He checks the crops and he coordinates the harvest team. His role is critical for our quality reports."
JAMES: "This is exactly what I need for the board report. Thank you, Ana!"

He doesn't / She doesn't + Advérbios de Frequência

Hoje você aprende a dizer o que alguém NÃO faz e a usar always/usually/sometimes/never para dar mais detalhes sobre a rotina.

📹 Vídeo aula incluído 📖 8 palavras novas ⏱ 25 minutos
▶ Assistir Vídeo — Dia 2
🔄
Contraste — Negativa na 3ª pessoa
He doesn't / She doesn't — nunca He don't!
I/You → don't · He/She → doesn't · Verbo sempre base!
I don't manage.→He doesn't manage.
You don't travel.→She doesn't travel.
I don't work at the office.→He doesn't work at the office.
He manages. (afirm.)→He doesn't manage. (neg.)
Erro clássico: "He doesn't manages" — ERRADO! Com doesn't, o verbo volta para a forma base: He doesn't manage. Nunca -S depois de doesn't!

Desafio de Contraste — Dia 3
Does he/she? vs. Is he/she? — qual usar?
Ação → DOES HE/SHE? · Identidade/Status → IS HE/SHE?
Does he manage the team?/Is he the manager?
Does she travel to farms?/Is she at the farm?
Does he coordinate logistics?/Is he available?
Does she handle reports?/Is she the coordinator?
Regra rápida: Se a resposta é uma AÇÃO → Does he/she? Se a resposta é quem/como/onde ELE/ELA É → Is he/she?
